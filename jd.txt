Role: GTM Engineering Intern

You will leverage LLMs, AI agents, automation tools, APIs, and data systems to improve efficiency, personalization, decision-making, and growth across platforms such as Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Meta, Shiprocket, Delhivery, and other e-commerce channels.

Responsibilities:
- Design and develop AI-powered workflows across marketing, sales, operations, and content.
- Build internal tools and automations that enable teams to execute faster and with greater precision.
- Develop AI agents and automated workflows to reduce manual work across teams.
- Automate repetitive marketing, operations, analytics, and reporting tasks.
- Create dashboards and monitoring systems that surface actionable insights.
- Analyze customer journeys across ecommerce and digital platforms.

Key Requirements:
- Strong interest in AI, automation, growth, and technology.
- Understanding of APIs, data workflows, and automation platforms.
- Ability to learn quickly and experiment independently.
- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.

Preferred Skills:
- Experience with LLMs such as GPT, Claude, Gemini, or open-source models.
- Understanding of e-commerce ecosystems and marketplace operations.
- Experience with SQL, dashboards, analytics, or data visualization tools.
- Previous projects involving AI agents, automation, or growth engineering.
